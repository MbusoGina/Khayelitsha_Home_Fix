## Project Overview:
Khayelitsha HomeFix is a fictional small business created for the WEDE5020 website project. The proposed organisation provides practical home-maintenance services to households in Khayelitsha and surrounding Cape Town areas.

The website is designed as a five-page small/medium business website:
1. Home (`index.html`)
2. About Us (`about.html`)
3. Services (`services.html`)
4. Enquiry (`enquiry.html`)
5. Contact (`contact.html`)

## Website Goals and Objectives
- Establish a clear online presence for the proposed business.
- Explain services in a simple, scannable format.
- Make it easy for visitors to request an enquiry or quote.
- Provide contact information and two proposed service-area map points.
- Build trust through clear branding, realistic business information and transparent communication.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Monthly website visits.
- Enquiry form submissions.
- Clicks on telephone/WhatsApp contact links.
- Clicks on the services page.
- Contact-page visits and map interactions.
- Mobile usability and page-load performance.
